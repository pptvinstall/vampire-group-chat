One character at a time, in this order. Daymondre is the style anchor: attach his approved new sheet with every sheet after him.

## 1. Daymondre

Attach: `01_cast/old_refs/Daymondre_V9_sheet.png`.

```text
CHARACTER SHEET — Daymondre (1 of 6)
Update to the kickoff: we are making the six character sheets here first, one at a time. A prompt labelled CHARACTER SHEET is the one exception to rule 5.

ATTACHED: Daymondre_V9_sheet.png (old reference for Daymondre).
Use the old reference for identity only: face shape, features, skin tone, hair and build. Redraw Daymondre in our locked look: Grounded, expressive, cinematic stylized 3D animation. Soft geometric faces, expressive eyes, smooth but real skin shading, believable fabric. Not photoreal, not live-action, not anime, not flat illustration.
This sheet sets the look for the whole cast, so get the shading and proportions right here.

WHO: Black man, Stefon's older brother with a clearly different face, short curly taper, goatee, black leather jacket, thick gold bat pendant on a chain, smug half-smile.
OUTFIT (the only one, worn all episode): Black leather jacket over a black top, thick gold chain with a gold bat pendant, black pants, black boots.

LAYOUT: one landscape 3:2 image on a plain mid-gray studio background.
- Left: full body, front view, relaxed neutral stance.
- Center: large face close-up, neutral expression.
- Right: three smaller full-body views: three-quarter, side and back.
- Bottom row: five head-and-shoulders expressions: smug half-smile, amused grin, bored shrug, cold stare, delighted.
- One extra head close-up at the far right of the bottom row: his vampire face. Exact same face and goatee, with red-amber irises, faint dark veins under both eyes and two small fangs. No blood.

RULES:
- The same face, hair and outfit in every panel.
- No text, labels, names, captions or logos anywhere on the sheet.
- An original character. Not based on any real actor or existing show.
- Daymondre is the only person in leather and the only one with the gold bat pendant. He and Stefon are brothers, never twins.
- Drop the toothpick from the old reference.
```
