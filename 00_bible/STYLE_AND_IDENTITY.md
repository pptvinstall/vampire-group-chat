## Visual settings
- ONE primary generation model for all dialogue and motion: no alternating between multiple video generators in the final cut. Reuse the same face-anchor crop and style prefix in every prompt. An exception requires a new visual match test.
- One master: vertical 9:16, 1080×1920 final deliverable, 30 fps. AI may generate at 720p; upscale uniformly at export.
- Camera language: restrained 35mm-equivalent medium shots, 50mm over-shoulder dialogue, 85mm-equivalent close-ups; consistent lens treatment, moderate depth of field. No random whip zooms, 360-degree spins, jump cuts inside generated shots, fisheye lenses, or disorienting angle changes.
- Color pipeline: filmic warmth in skin tones, soft gold daylight at the school; amber + burgundy at Creekside Grill; cool deep-blue fill with warm practical parking-lot lights at night. Match contrast and saturation across cuts; consistent grade in editing. No arbitrary color swapping between models.
- Lighting continuity: early morning school; **same day, after school** at the grill; **same evening** turning into night outside. Evening-to-night transition must be an establishing insert before Vanesha leaves.
- Fixed sign/props: SHADOW CREEK HIGH on school entrance; CREEKSIDE GRILL on the same brick storefront; same booth, same counter, same parking-lot arrangement every time. Signage added in editing where AI render fails, not improvised per clip.
- Audio: no AI-generated spoken dialogue used as final audio. Build one locked voice per actor, record every line on separate voice tracks with consistent performance; then lip-sync dialogue close-ups against those exact voice files. Sounds, music, and group chat typography added in post. No narrator.
- Background music: one original understated contemporary Atlanta-influenced score bed, no commercial samples or recognizable songs; warm/laid-back at school/grill, darker sub bass at night.
- On-screen texting: never ask video generators to spell actual messages. Composite all exact text with one UI template in post.
