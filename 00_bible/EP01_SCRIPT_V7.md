**Story:** Eiesha wants one quiet first day back after losing her parents. She meets the strange new boy, Stefon, at school; their after-school meal at Creekside Grill turns dangerous when his older brother Daymondre arrives. The town's group chat spreads the alarm before either brother can cover up what happened.
