# CONTINUITY + STORY REPAIR NOTES · EP01 V7

## Why this revision is cleaner
OLD: 18 story scenes, 22 video snippets, five-plus disparate settings, a disconnected family-kitchen storyline, unexplained mansion jumps, an attack, and a late group-chat montage.
NEW: 12 scenes, one real day, three connected sets (**school → Creekside Grill → its parking lot / back exit**), one central mystery, and one escalating cause-and-effect chain.

**Cause and effect:** Eiesha wants a normal day → notices Stefon → his impossible reflexes alarm Bianca → they go to Creekside → Daymondre follows Stefon → he targets Vanesha → Marquell finds his sister and alerts the chat → Eiesha looks for Stefon → the vampire secret spills in front of her.

## Character motives
- Eiesha: wants routine and connection while grieving; curiosity drives her into danger.
- Bianca: cares about Eiesha, detects danger first, calls for help instead of being magically correct all the time.
- Stefon: genuinely wants a normal life, but his older brother disrupts it; he tries to protect people.
- Daymondre: comes to town to provoke Stefon, pushes boundaries, ends episode exposed to people who won't keep quiet.
- Marquell: protective brother and reliable worker, not just the jealous ex. He finds Vanesha and starts the emergency response.
- Vanesha: funny and independent; she is not disposable comic relief. She survives, and her memory matters next episode.

## Story continuity rules
1. Everything happens on one day. School is morning. The grill is after school into dinner. The parking lot is after dark. Use the SAME grill storefront and side door, including the same exit into the parking lot.
2. Marquell wears work uniform at Creekside, even though his character sheet also has varsity outfits. No instant outfit changes.
3. Stefon looks like an actual student with his blue-gray overshirt. Daymondre wears black leather and the gold bat chain. They are clearly brothers, never twins, and always Black.
4. Vanesha leaves Creekside, encounters Daymondre and is attacked without a graphic wound. She is then found near the car by Marquell. No teleporting between scenes or unexplained location changes.
5. Daymondre returns to the grill's **back service exit** after the attack. Stefon confronts him there, and Eiesha/Bianca follow to find Stefon, overhearing the conversation. We do not jump to the mansion.
6. The chat message follows Marquell finding Vanesha, not before. Kari participates through text only. Emergency help is called.
7. Keep a single wardrobe and prop look throughout, including Eiesha's braids, Bianca's pendant, Stefon's glasses, Marquell's apron, Vanesha's jacket, Daymondre's chain.
8. Spoken lines performed in **separate mono voice tracks per character**; locked voice IDs, no generator-improvised cross-character voice switching. If visual lip-sync fails, use an over-shoulder or reaction view and add correct audio in the edit.
9. No new speaking characters. All other approved cast members remain available for future episodes.
10. The vampire reveal must look scary for two seconds before the final AP History joke. Sound and pacing preserve the threat.
